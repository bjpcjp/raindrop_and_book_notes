Better America / Better World

A beacon from the American South and a bellwether for the nation, The Bitter Southerner is an Athens, Ga.-based independent media company, founded in 2013, that connects an activated and vocal global community working to make the South, and America, a better place. Today, The Bitter Southerner publishes three print magazines annually, books under its BS Publishing imprint, records at their new BS Records label, the “Batch” podcast, and offers iconic apparel and home goods in the BS General Store.
