I Want To Eat Your Cereal!

I want to eat your cereal! Count Chocula isn’t your average vampire, he is a chocolate loving, cereal eating vampire! Introduced in 1971, this seasonal cereal was seen in the fall with other Monster cereals like the strawberry flavored Franken Berry. The Count shown here is very retro, and printed on a Charcoal Heather tee with a light distress over the print. You may become nostalgic while wearing this tee, so next fall stock up on the chocolaty cereal because this shirt is NOT edible or scratch n’ sniff!

Our team paid tribute to this sweet-toothed vampire with his very own retro graphic tee featuring the classic Count Chocula cereal box logo. To complete the vintage look and feel on this Count Chocula shirt - we've distressed the entire graphic and printed it on the highest quality, poly-cotton Charcoal Heather tee around.
