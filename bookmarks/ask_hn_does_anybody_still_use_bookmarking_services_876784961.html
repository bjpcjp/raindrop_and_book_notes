  • one-click saving

    Bookmark (we call it "historify") your favorite sites with just a single click. No categories to select or forms to fill out, and it works on all browsers!

  • full-text search

    Search your historified sites using a simple search box. Don't wade through lists of obscure titles, enter a few keywords you remember from the content of the page and you're off!

  • page snapshots

    historious always keeps a cached version of the page you historified, just as it was when you saw it!

  • share with the world

    historious creates your personalised search engine containing the sites you specify. Just give your friends the address and they can search your items!
