Here are academic theories about how we pay attention to things.

  • Attachment Theory: we attach to others in reciprocal and primitive ways.
  • Confirmation Bias: we seek confirmation that we made a good decision.
  • Inattentional Blindness: attention to one thing makes us miss another.
  • Information Processing Theory: persuasion requires attention and comparison with previous views.
  • Ironic Reversal: we cannot avoid thinking about things we want to avoid thinking about.
  • Selective Exposure: we avoid what will cause dissonance about a decision.
