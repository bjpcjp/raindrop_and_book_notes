For more than thirty years, Ed Binkley has been producing stunning fantasy artwork. His exceptional creations have been showcased in a multitude of publications and he has partnered with esteemed entities such as Lucasfilm, ImagineFX, Wizards of the Coast, and Artifacts Puzzles, among others.

Binkley is recognized for his highly detailed art pieces that possess both whimsical and eerie qualities. Frequently, he showcases peculiar characters and anthropomorphic beings with exaggerated features. He recently unveiled an art book that exhibits a compilation of colored pencil drawings from his “Incognito” collection.
