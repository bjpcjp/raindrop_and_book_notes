PITTSBURGH'S HOME FOR GOURMET COFFEE AND TEA

Located in the heart of Pittsburgh's historic Strip District, Allegheny Coffee & Tea Exchange is your location for the best coffee in the ‘burgh. We offer an exceptional selection of the highest-quality coffees and teas from around the world, available by the cup or by the pound, and are unique in our in-house production of nitro coffee. Beans are roasted on site in small batches, and we select and blend the finest teas. Purchase your favorites online, or visit us in store for a drink and browse in person. This is where your perfect cup begins.

Draft Coffee on Tap
