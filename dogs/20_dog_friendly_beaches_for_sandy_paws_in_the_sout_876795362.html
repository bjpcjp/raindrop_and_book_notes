20 Dog-Friendly Beaches For Sandy Paws In The South

URL: https://www.southernliving.com/dog-friendly-beaches-6529812

Tags: dogs, travel

Bookmark ID: 876795362

When it's time to pack your bags for a much-needed holiday, there's always the question of what to do with your pets. If you bring them along, then you'll need to make the extra effort to find pet-friendly accommodations and activities. If you decide to leave them, then you're tasked with finding a doggy daycare that won't cost an arm and a leg or asking friends and family for help.

To help solve this conundrum, we've rounded up some dog-friendly beaches across the South, from Alabama to Maryland, where your dog is welcome. While some beaches have leash restrictions, you'll never have to leave your pup at home with these destinations. Many allow furry friends to romp freely during the off-season or post specific times of day when pets are allowed without restraint. Be sure to keep in mind rules concerning dog etiquette when planning your trip, especially between Memorial Day and Labor Day, for a stress-free beach vacation.

01 of 20

Isle of Palms, South Carolina

Just northeast of Charleston, Isle of Palms allows beach-loving mutts to romp sans leash year-round with varying hours: September 15 to March 31 between 4 p.m. and 10 a.m. and then from 5 a.m. to 9 a.m. starting on April 1.

02 of 20

Oak Island, North Carolina

While pups are permitted year-round with a tether on Oak Island, you can let them splash freely from October 15 to March 15. Just be sure to check beach access signs for additional information.

03 of 20

Fort De Soto Park, Florida

Southwest of St. Petersburg is Fort De Soto Park which includes fenced-in sections of white sand that designate areas where pets are allowed, in addition to two dog parks. Here you can enjoy beach plants, mangroves, wetlands, and over 328 species of birds.

04 of 20

Dauphin Island, Alabama

What better way to relax than with a trip to the Gulf Coast with your furry friend? Dogs are welcome on the famed white sand beaches of Dauphin Island year-round, but must be on a leash at all times and picked up after. Spend the afternoon dipping your toes (and paws) into the warm waters before exploring everything the island has to offer.

05 of 20

Ocean Springs, Mississippi

In the coastal town of Ocean Springs, you can play fetch with your pet on East Beach all afternoon. However, if you feel like visiting Front Beach, your dog will need to be on a leash. After you've had your fun in the sun, take time to explore the quaint city.

06 of 20

Virginia Beach, Virginia

Come have some fun in Virginia Beach with a stroll down Atlantic Avenue and a day on the sand. Pets are welcome here, but be mindful of restricted hours in the summer season on the beach and boardwalk. Throw in a snow cone, hot dog, and surf outing, and it's a day.

07 of 20

Edisto Island Beach, South Carolina

On Edisto Beach, let your pets run as they please from November 1 through April 30. Even if your dog does not require a leash, you must carry one in your hand while your pet is running free. Animals are required to be on leash May 1 to October 31.

08 of 20

Jupiter, Florida

Jupiter's dog beach area has been pup friendly since 1989 and is one of the few off-leash, dog-friendly beaches in the state. The designated 2.5-mile stretch from marker #26 to #57 invites animals to frolic without restraint.

09 of 20

St. Simons Island East Beach, Georgia

You can access to St. Simons Island's East Beach via Massengale Park. Adjacent to the relaxed shore, you'll find free parking and plenty of shaded picnic areas. Between Memorial Day and Labor Day, animals are prohibited on the beach, except after 6 p.m. and before 9 a.m. when they are welcomed with or without a leash.

10 of 20

Rutherford Beach, Lousiana

Rutherford Beach, a locals' favorite at the mouth of the Mermentau River, is not only a treasure trove for shell collecting, but the quiet coastline has ample room for pooches to play.

11 of 20

Myrtle Beach, South Carolina

The famous Myrtle Beach boasts 60 miles of coastline, including Garden City Beach, Surfside Beach, Atlantic Beach, and North Myrtle Beach. Dogs are welcome on the beaches, boardwalk, and promenade, but take care to abide by the guidelines and hours. From May 1 through Labor Day, pups can come before 10 a.m. and after 5 p.m. The rest of the year leashed dogs are allowed on the beach anytime of day.

12 of 20

Bonita Springs, Florida

A little bit farther south from St. Petersburg, Bonita Springs Beach Dog Park is another gem that allows dogs to run without a leash. It even provides a station to rinse off dirty paws after playing in the sand and offers free parking.

13 of 20

Padre Island National Seashore, Texas

You can take your dog to Port Aransas for some weekend fun, but they must be kept on a leash. One particularly beautiful spot to visit, in addition to the pier, is the Padre Island National Seashore—not to be confused with South Padre Island. Located just outside Corpus Christi, there are 70 miles of undeveloped beaches and natural habitat to explore.

14 of 20

Pawleys Island, South Carolina

In this hidden gem of a town, dogs are allowed on the beach year-round. If your pet is well trained with voice command, they are free to roam without a leash as long as the owner has one in hand; however, during the busy season (May 1 to September 30) animals must be restrained between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m..

15 of 20

Driftwood Beach, Georgia

Jekyll Island welcomes dogs on a leash. The majestic and mysterious, Driftwood Beach, which is consistently named one of the most romantic beaches in the country, offers plenty to explore.

16 of 20

St. Andrews Beach, Georgia

A stone's throw from Driftwood Beach is St. Andrews Beach, where you can observe migratory birds and dolphins from the two-story wildlife viewing platform. Pets must be kept on a leash at all times.

17 of 20

Ocean City Beach, Maryland

From October through April, dogs on a leash are welcome on the beach and three-mile boardwalk in Ocean City Beach, which has been named one of TripAdvisor's top 10 beaches.

18 of 20

Mustang Island State Park, Texas

Dog owners will find a pet-friendly area at Mustang Island State Park. Make sure your furry friend is on a leash, and the two of you are free to roam the five miles of coastline. Visitors typically enjoy sunbathing, fishing, and birdwatching.

19 of 20

Brunswick Islands, North Carolina

Pets are welcome on all Brunswick Islands beaches. Be sure to check beach access signs for additional information. Before you go, know the rules and hours when pets are allowed (on and off the leash) during summer and off season. Here, you can see wildlife, experience small-town charm, and find seclusion—perfect for a little getaway.

20 of 20

Matapeake Beach, Maryland

About an hour east of Washington, D.C., on the Chesapeake Bay, Kent Island is home to Matapeake Beach. Follow a short trail to an area separate from the main public beach access called "Dog Beach" where dogs can swim and roam without a leash.
