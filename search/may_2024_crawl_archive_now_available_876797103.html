The crawl archive for May 2024 is now available. This is our 100th crawl! 🎉

The data was crawled between May 18th and May 31st, and contains 2.7 billion web pages (or 377 TiB of uncompressed content). Page captures are from 50.3 million hosts or 39.6 million registered domains and include 0.9 billion new URLs, not visited in any of our prior crawls.

Archive Location & Download

The May 2024 crawl archive is located in the commoncrawl bucket at crawl-data/CC-MAIN-2024-22/.

To assist with exploring and using the dataset, we provide gzipped files which list all segments, WARC, WAT and WET files.

By simply adding either s3://commoncrawl/ or https://data.commoncrawl.org/ to each line, you end up with the S3 and HTTP paths respectively, please see Get Started for detailed instructions.
