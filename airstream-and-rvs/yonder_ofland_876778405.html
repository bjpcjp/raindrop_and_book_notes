First, and most importantly: we haven't sold the park, the team is the same, and you'll still have the same great experience when you visit us next.

Our new name, Ofland, is based on our simple ethos: to be "of the land."

Since we opened in 2021, we have learned so much by carefully listening to your feedback and by watching you enjoy the hotel. We've made many changes – some small, like tweaking our food & beverage offerings, and some major, such as adding deluxe cabins that have private en suite bathrooms.

When we launched Yonder Escalante, our focus was building a luxury RV park that had a handful of rooms. Our most important lesson over the last few years has come from watching you have incredible experiences in the great outdoors. Seeing this has made us realize that our primary goal is to provide an exceptional outdoor hotel experience that helps you connect with nature and each other. 

Many of you have asked where and when we’re opening our next hotel. (We'll have some exciting news soon!) While searching the US for perfect locations that live up to what we have in Escalante, we realized that duplicating what we’ve done here would be a disservice to the uniqueness of the new sites. As such, we’ve adjusted our strategy and design to ensure that what we're building next is not merely on the land, but rather of the land.

Given the new growth we’re working towards, we wanted to ensure all of our properties bear the Ofland name, making now the perfect time to officially change our name from Yonder Escalante to Ofland Escalante. 

Don't worry, the park will feel the same as it did the last time you were here. We are right where you left us in Escalante, excited to welcome you back with warm smiles!

We truly couldn’t have made it this far without you and from the bottom of our hearts, thank you so much for your support. We're excited to embark on this new chapter together! 

Here's to Ofland - safe travels and we'll see you soon!
