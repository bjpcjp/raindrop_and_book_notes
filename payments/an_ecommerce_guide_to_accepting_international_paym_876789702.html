By Anna Smith, freelance writer.

Pursuing global markets can be a thrilling and significant step for your business. However, it’d be best to establish a secure and proficient means for accepting payments internationally. The best worldwide payment method for your eCommerce will differ depending on the location or country of your customers.

If you choose to accept international payments, cautiously research all the facts, like their local banking structure, and select a safe payment method that enables you to get paid easily and quickly. Carefully researching your overseas market, payment options you'll need, and other difficulties you might meet will increase the probability of your business thriving.

International payments can be complex compared to receiving payments from your local customers. Select a payment option that works best for your eCommerce business. Wire transfers work best when you're working with customers who make big payments. It's also essential to consider transaction fees, whether it's conversion, transfer, processing, or any other applicable fee. Another factor you need to consider is speed; pay attention to how fast you can withdraw your money. With that said, here’s a quick guide on how you can accept international payments for your eCommerce business.

1. Create A Secure Online Payment Gateway

To accept international payments, you must create a payment gateway that presents a wide range of payment methods and safely processes your prospect's payment information. There are a few ways you can create a safe online payment gateway. You could hire an external developer or use your website development employees to build a tailored gateway. Also, third-party software could be helpful.

If your eCommerce shop is still new, choose a one-in-all payment method like PayPal or eBay. This payment platform allows you to receive, hold and convert money in foreign currencies. You only need to open a foreign currency account and integrate it with your online store's payment gateway. Also, such payment options minimize the hassle of online transactions by presenting fast, safe, and user-friendly monetary solutions. The more payment methods you offer in your online payment portal, the broader the market and the easier it gets for your customers to make payments. The most commonly used online payment methods by eCommerce stores include:

  • Credit and debit cards: It's essential to allow your customers to pay for products or services using debit or credit cards because most online customers expect this from all online shops. The most popular cards used are MasterCard and Visa. It's even better if your payment system supports more brands.
  • Digital wallets: With this payment method, you and your customers need to have the same payment service provider. Then, connect both your accounts to your digital wallets to deposit and withdraw money. Digital wallets are gradually becoming common; therefore, it’d be best to offer this payment method for your customer's convenience.
  • Bank transfers: Most people still prefer this traditional money transfer method. Hence, it’d be great if your payment system supported it. You also need to give your customers your banking detail.

Developing a safe and sound gateway is crucial. You're also setting up computerized processes in a position that will save you the time and effort of manual processing, particularly as you grow your online business and start making large transactions.

2. Set Up Recurring Billing

Recurring billing enables you to establish an automatic charging sequence for your customers, making it a prerequisite for your online business if you offer services with weekly, monthly, or annual payment plans. For example, most video-streaming platforms offer their services on a monthly subscription basis. Furthermore, NGOs have found recurring billing convenient, allowing organizations to assemble money from their usual donors quickly.

The majority of payment processing software also has recurring billing aspects. Some sites provide all the requirements to set up a customized automated recurring billing in case you have an existing payment processing system. Automation is crucial to your online business, as it eliminates the risks of errors and the constant worry of following up on invoices and payments. To achieve this, your customers only need to accept recurring payments with just a couple of clicks, and you won't have to be bothered about typically handling your client base.

3. Mobile Payments

Recently, most people are more likely to have or carry their smartphones at hand than credit or debit cards. In addition, mobile payment applications have become more reliable than ever. Online payment methods like Venmo, Google Pay, and PayPal also have mobile applications with a respectable market share.

With mobile payments, your customers can transfer money via their phones with leading brand apps or a mobile-enhanced website.

4. Use Email Invoicing

Email invoicing is a hands-on method to accept payments from your overseas clients. You can add a link to your payment gateway that’ll redirect the customer there or share a payment document via email. Although it's a crucial part of online payment processing for many eCommerce businesses worldwide, email isn't the most reliable means of communication. Therefore, most online buyers have trust issues with this payment method.

5. Accept Electronic Checks (eChecks)

If you want your customers to use eChecks for payments, create a platform where the client can easily key in their info, which you can notice via a payment processing app or software. It's an approach customers use to pay for purchases by check through the internet. Electronic checks are faster and more dependable than sending a manual check via the post, so using this in your international payment system will make the process easy without any hitches.

6. Accept Cryptocurrency Payments

Accepting cryptocurrencies is one of the best ways to widen your reach to an extensive online market. Some sites have all the tools you'll require to agree to online crypto purchases, request payments, mail invoices, and collect funds on run-through applications.

Cryptocurrency involves worldwide trade and exchange. Therefore, it may present exceptional benefits to your online business. You can agree to payments from anywhere globally without attracting currency conversion, bank, or transaction fees. There's also a minimized chance of fraud.

7. Use Payment Links

Payment links are one of the most convenient ways for your customers to make purchases. These links are safe, shared URLs or QR codes that you can link to your preferred payment platforms. You can add links to texts, emails, messenger, or chat apps. Consider directly inserting them into invoices or quotes for quicker payments.

Conclusion

Regardless of which international payment method or software you accept, the most crucial thing is that it should be secure, reliable, and easy for your prospects to make purchases. Additionally, the more methods or options they can use, the more possibility your customers can carry through on a purchase. Many methods allow you to accept international payments online. Your aim should be to present various payment options that meet your explicit business desires and the needs of your prospects.
