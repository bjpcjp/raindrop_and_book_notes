API Solution

Automating the purchasing procedure saves you time, enables you to go to market faster, and makes your business more efficient by using our range of digital capabilities to your advantage. APIs (Application Programming Interfaces) connect your day-to-day computer systems and processes with DigiKey’s expansive data and functionality.

DigiKey offers a wide range of APIs at no cost.

  • Receive Real-Time Price and Availability

  • Place an Order
  • View Order Status and Change Orders
  • Integrate with myLists
  • Manage Supply Chain Program
