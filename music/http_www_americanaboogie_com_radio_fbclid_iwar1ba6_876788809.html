Tubby Tunes Radio Show (Sunday, 8 AM PST/11 AM EST/3 PM GMT)
Tubby Tunes radio show is an eclectic music blend hosted by DJ Long John. Keeping the spirit of KFAT-Gilroy alive & well, he’s always finding interesting, and sometimes rare cuts to play on the radio. It’s an entertaining high calorie version of Americana type radio, usually upbeat and never to be confused with Top 40 sugar pop country radio. Try it, you’ll like it

How to listen to ABR on Amazon Echo, Dot and other “Alexa” devices.

There is now a very simple way to listen to ABR on your “Alexa” device. Just say “Alexa, play Americana Boogie Radio” when ever you want to listen!

OR, you can also enable the Live365 skill by simply saying “Alexa, Enable Live365 Skill.”After enabling the Live365 skill, tune into Live365 by saying “Alexa, play Americana Boogie Radio on Live365!”This will also work for “myTuner Radio” if you enable it in Amazon.
Please email me if you have any problems or if you have found other ways to listen on Alexa.

ABR is also on Live365, Streema Radio, RadioFM and OnLine Radio Box, InternetRadio

How to request a song on Americana Boogie Radio…
