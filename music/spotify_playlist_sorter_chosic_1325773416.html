Sort your Spotify playlist by BPM, artist, genres, moods, and more musical attributes. Organize and manage your playlist however you like! The idea of this tool is to give you more options to reorder your playlist than those available on Spotify. You can also remove duplicates from your playlists or like all tracks in any of your playlists with just one click.

With the Spotify Playlist Sorter tool, get your playlists in order by sorting them by genre, mood, and much more.

How to sort my playlist order using Spotify app?

In the Spotify app, after selecting a playlist, there is a dropdown list in the right corner with a limited number of options (Title, Artist, Album, Date added, Duration) to sort your playlist. You can also drag and drop the songs to get the order you want. These options are limited, and the “drag and drop” feature is not practical for those who have hundreds of songs in their playlists.

Here you are, this tool is here to help you. It provides you with many options to sort your music by various musical attributes.

How to use the Spotify Playlist Sorter tool?

You can easily reorder the songs in any of your playlists by a wide range of parameters. Just follow these steps:

  1. Log in with your Spotify account. Once you’re logged in, your music library will be synced.
  2. Choose one of your playlists (to help you easily find the playlist you want, you can sort your Spotify playlists by clicking on headers. This sort will not change your playlists on Spotify).
  3. The playlist result table will be shown. Click on the column headings in the playlist table to sort the playlist the way you want.
  4. Save the new sorted playlist to Spotify. It also lets you pick songs to make a new playlist from.

Sort your playlists

This music playlist sorter was built using the Spotify API. It lets you get your playlists in order based on genres, moods, and various musical attributes, including:

  • Popularity (High-Low and vice versa)
  • Track Length (Long-short and vice versa)
  • Danceability (High-Low and vice versa)
  • Energy (High-Low and vice versa)
  • Acousticness (High-Low and vice versa)
  • Instrumentalness (High-Low and vice versa)
  • Valence (High-Low and vice versa)
  • Speechiness (High-Low and vice versa)
  • Liveness (High-Low and vice versa)
  • Loudness (High-Low and vice versa)
  • Tempo (BPM) (High-Low and vice versa)
  • Key – Music Key for this song (High-Low and vice versa)
  • Time Signature (High-Low and vice versa)

In addition, you can sort your playlist by

  1. Tracks (Alphabetical [A-Z] and vice versa)
  2. Artists (Alphabetical [A-Z] and vice versa)
  3. Main genres (Alphabetical [A-Z] and vice versa)
  4. Sub-genres (Alphabetical [A-Z] and vice versa)
  5. Albums (Alphabetical [A-Z] and vice versa)
  6. Release Date (Newest First or Oldest First)

This tool gives you more control over your playlists with three additional options:

1- Like all songs in Spotify playlist

You can add all playlist songs to your library (liked songs) with just one click.

2- Remove duplicates from Spotify playlist

This tool checks any playlists in your Spotify library to find duplicates. Once it finds them, you can show and remove them.

3- Clean your Spotify playlists

Make your playlist clean! Bulk delete unwanted songs from your playlist, just select them, then click “delete selected songs” in the dropdown button to remove multiple tracks at once while keeping the chosen order.

Notes:

  • Playlist Sorter is NOT available for huge playlists, so playlists with more than 3000 tracks will not show automatically.
  • No changes happen to your Spotify library until you explicitly click on “Like all songs” or “Save as new playlists” buttons. The “Save as new playlists” button creates a new playlist in your library and does not affect your pre-existing playlists.
  • Your Spotify data is private and safe; it will not be stored.
  • If you want to rate or analyze the playlist or organize it by genres, moods, or decades, use our Spotify Playlist Analyzer tool
  • If you want to find similar songs based on your playlist, try Similar Songs Finder tool.
