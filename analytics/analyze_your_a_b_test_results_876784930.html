Importance of Statistical Significance

So you’ve split tested your landing pages and realized that A is better than B. How do you determine whether or not this was a chance occurrence? For example, what if it rained every time you wore a green shirt in a given month? Would you conclude that wearing a green shirt brings rain?

This is why it is important to consider statistical significance when doing AB testing. Statistical significance is a term used in statistical computations to determine the likelihood of a result being a random occurrence. It is usually expressed as a percentage. A significance test score of 5% (or a 95% confidence level) is considered acceptable. This means that the chance of the result being random is very small. However, anything above 5% shows that the result is a chance occurrence.

Statistical significance is calculated using the number of conversions and the number of visitors in each group. Here is a great guide on how to calculate statistical significance. Statistical significance gives a proof beyond doubt that you’re A/B test results are not a chance occurrence. This is why any AB test calculator should test for significance.
