“Dimensionality reduction” is a trending keyword in the Google search engine. This is because the whole machine learning is full of dimensionality reduction and its applications. It is time to see them in action with examples!

Dimensionality reduction is an unsupervised machine learning technique that can be applied to your input data, without having a label column. In technical terms, the number of variables (also known as features or attributes) in your data is called the dimensionality of data. If your data has 3 variables, the dimensionality of your data is 3. It means that any point in your data can be plotted in a 3-dimensional space.

Real-world data has many variables meaning that its dimensionality is very high. Commonly, such data is referred to as n-dimensional data. The “n” is equal to the number of variables and it can be 10, 100, 1000 or even more than 10,000.
