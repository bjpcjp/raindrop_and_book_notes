OpenAI's president Greg Brockman recently shared this cool template for prompting their reasoning models o1/o3. Turns out, this is great for ANY reasoning model, incl. Grok 3 and DeepSeek. Here's how it works: Instead of rigid step-by-step instructions, structure your prompt like this: ✅ GOAL: What do you want the model to achieve? ✅ RETURN FORMAT: How should the output be structured? ✅ WARNINGS: What should the model be careful of or avoid? ✅ CONTEXT DUMP: Any relevant details, references, or knowledge sources. Now pay attention to what's NOT listed here: A precise task description telling the model exactly HOW to do the job. Why? Because reasoning models excel at ARRIVING at a solution, not FOLLOWING a specific path. Over-explaining often leads to worse results and is usually a sign that you don't need a reasoning model at all. Just a "normal" GPT, or whatever you want to call it.
