Improve ML Outcomes with Quality Image Annotation

Getting images annotated according to your specifications can be a challenge that slows your machine learning or deep learning project and, as a result, your speed to market. The choices you make about your image annotation techniques, tools, and workforce are worth thoughtful consideration.

In this guide, you’ll learn:

  • The basics of image annotation for computer vision using supervised learning
  • Techniques that are available for annotating visual data, including images and videos
  • Considerations for selecting the right workforce
  • Key questions to ask a potential image annotation service provider
