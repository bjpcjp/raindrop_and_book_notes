Over two years ago, I made the life-changing decision of losing weight and getting ripped — it took me six months of grueling hard work to lose 55 lbs and get a visible six-pack.

But I almost killed myself and lost quite some muscle and strength in the process.

Thanks to my half-baked research, I had made a lot of costly mistakes. I’ve learned a lot since then and a testament to this is the ease with which I stay lean year-round.

In the early days post my weight loss, I stuck to a stringent diet, obsessively counted every single calorie, constantly checked my abs in the mirror, worked them out every day, and did hours of cardio.

I might’ve looked great physically, but mentally, I felt like shit.

I do none of those things now but still sport a visible six-pack. I neither have a diet nor do I count calories. I go out to eat. I drink occasionally. I hate ab exercises, so I don’t do them. I mostly eat what I want. And I don’t do any cardio.
