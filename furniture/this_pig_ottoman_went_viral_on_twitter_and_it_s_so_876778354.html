“No thoughts just pig ottoman” is Twitter user @2Saddington’s quarantine mood — and it’s a relatable one at that. This cute little footstool from Target went viral on March 3 with over 100,000 retweets.

The Burton New Velvet Pig Ottoman could be yours for less than $60 — though certain colorways in some areas are, unsurprisingly, sold out after the footstool’s five minutes of fame.

IMO, if you’re the current owner of the one-of-a-kind pig sofa chair, you need the Target footstool to match. And if you’re not the owner of the chair — or a fan of pigs in general — fear not, there are other adorable animal footstools available.

And while you could put these ottomans in any room, they do look pretty adorable in nurseries. Whether your ottoman is a grown-up purchase or a surprise for your kiddo, it’ll add a bit of whimsy and fun to your space.
